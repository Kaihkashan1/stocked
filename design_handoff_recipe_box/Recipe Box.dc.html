Nine directions

No letterform in any. Each is shown large and at 60px, the size it actually lives at on a home screen.

A Lidded pot — the box, steaming
B Box of cards, steam rising out of it
C Pot and plates — cooking and eating
D Outline — drawn, not filled
E Top-down pot — rings, reads at any size
F Steam only — the most abstract
G Place setting, steaming — cooking and eating
H Bowl with a spoon in it, steam above
I Half pot, half plate — split down the middle

G–I put cooking and eating in one mark: a steaming plate with fork and spoon, a bowl with the spoon still in it, and a split frame with the pot on one side and the plate on the other. G reads fastest; H is the calmest; I is the boldest but busiest at 60px.

A–C are solid silhouettes, D is the same pot drawn as an outline, E is a pot from above in flat rings, F drops the object entirely and keeps only steam over a lid line. E and F hold up best at 60px; C carries the most meaning and loses the most. Say which and I'll export it at every iOS size.
